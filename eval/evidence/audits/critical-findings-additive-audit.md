## Decision rule

A critical safety finding was retained only if readable evidence affirmatively established wrong identity, residential/personal use, or an unresolved *exclusive* assignment to another NPI/provider. Different professional contacts, shared facility/assistant lines, stale directory records, unreadable snapshots, and lower-tier disagreement were not treated as proof of unsafe disclosure.

## Root causes of the sealed overcalls

1. **Normalized-text loss despite a complete raw body.** H010's Florida government page was labeled a shell even though the retained raw HTML contained the complete facility JSON.
2. **Fetch unavailability was converted into contradiction.** H008, H025, and H053 exact returned pages were unavailable during sealed host fetching but are readable now and support the emitted facts.
3. **Difference was treated as exclusivity.** Several findings assumed that another phone/address or another NPI using a shared contact made the emitted value wrong. The source evidence did not establish exclusivity.
4. **Source hierarchy was applied before evidence qualification.** H025 is the clearest example: stale/different exact-NPI directories were allowed to overrule an exact-provider first-party page that directly supports the emitted values.
5. **The critical-contact category was applied to a case with no emitted contact.** BASELINE H053 emitted only specialty and website.
